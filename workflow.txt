PROJECT WORKFLOW

INTERACTIONS

ChoiceBridge
- ChoiceMaker
  - DialogContent
  - DialogFlag
  - FullArtContent
  - TransitionContent
- InteractPrompt

A ChoiceBridge can be placed on an item, NPC, or other interactable. It connects the prompt, available choices, and each choice's result.

ChoiceMaker
- Set the displayed label and optional icon.
- Add multiple ChoiceMakers for multiple options.
- Use flag conditions to show an option only when the required flags match.

DialogContent
- Add multiple dialogue lines and portraits in one resource.
- Set voice profiles and camera/player targets as needed.

DialogFlag
- Set a flag when an interaction completes.
- Optionally remove the used ChoiceMaker or its whole ChoiceBridge.

FullArtContent
- Set the image or animation and its accompanying lines.

Scene transitions
- Add TransitionContent to a ChoiceMaker.
- Set its destination scene and destination marker.
- Doorway transitions are handled in Scenes/Levels/level_scripts/level_transition.gd.

STORY AND BLOCKOUT NOTES

The original opening follows June finishing a late shift, going to her usual rooftop
smoking spot, then returning home. She must take her pills before she can sleep.
The theater idea may replace or reframe that opening; this is still undecided.

The theater acts as a hub. The ticket machine greets June, gives her a ticket, and
sends her to Screen 3. The factory chapter begins beyond that door.

CHAPTER 1: FACTORY

The factory is mostly ordinary and may represent denial. The player works through
a fuse-and-power sequence:
1. Place fuse 1 and use the lever to lower the ladder to the catwalk.
2. Find bolt cutters; the monitors still indicate insufficient power.
3. Place fuse 2 so the cameras work and reveal fuse 3.
4. Place fuse 3 to turn the roof-door light green.
5. Reach the roof and view the surreal landscape.

The roof view may include a distant flower field and gazebo, unusual fences, a large
anchor, a rift in the sky, floating land above water, and nearer geometric structures.
Keep the reveal visual; June does not need to explain it.

After the roof full-art moment, the door returns directly to the theater. The theater
and its NPCs can remain unchanged. The ticket machine can repeat its dialogue but
give June a crumpled ticket, which leads to the next screen.
